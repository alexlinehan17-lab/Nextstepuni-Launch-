**Subject:** Use of Examination Material Archive content in a free schools programme — NextStepUni

Dear State Examinations Commission,

I'm writing as the founder of NextStepUni, a free educational platform provided
to students in six DEIS schools in Dublin's North East Inner City as part of a
corporate-sponsored schools programme. Students never pay to use it.

NextStepUni already reproduces SEC examination materials (past-paper questions,

past-papers library inside the platform: a one-time mirror of the published
exam papers and marking schemes from your Exam Material Archive (Leaving
Certificate 2010–2025, Leaving Certificate Applied, and current-specification
Junior Cycle), so that students can read the official papers and schemes
free of charge on their phones. Specifically:

- The copy was made politely and identifiably (single-threaded, rate-limited,

apply any particular attribution wording, or has any concern with this use,
please contact me at this address and we will adjust promptly.

Thank you for maintaining the archive — it is an extraordinary public resource.
