# Evidence Dossier — Your Strategic Advantage

**Module:** `strategic-advantage-protocol` (`components/StrategicAdvantageModule.tsx`)
**Review date:** 2026-06-23
**Reviewer:** Pre-accreditation literature review (DCU / Brian MacCraith)
**Governing rule:** Only state/advise what peer-reviewed literature supports; every DOI
verified via CrossRef. References surface via inline `Cite` markers + the module-wide
**References** button; data in `data/references/strategicAdvantage.ts`.

**Reviewer note.** Two clean evidence bases. The first four sections are McAdams'
narrative-identity research — narrative identity, redemption vs contamination sequences,
the agency/communion thematic clusters, and pivotal scenes (McAdams & McLean 2013). The
fourth section reframes structural disadvantage through the learning-science of desirable
difficulties (Soderstrom & Bjork 2015) and the spacing effect — distributed practice
beats cramming (Cepeda et al. 2006). **No content cut.** The narrative claims are
described as McAdams' theory (which links redemptive narration to well-being), and the
disadvantage-as-training framing is positioned as motivational reframing grounded in
desirable-difficulty research, not a claim that disadvantage is good. The Narrative
Switcher, Agency/Communion Balancer and "Difficulty Dividend" chart are illustrative
interactions (the chart curves are stylised, not study data).

## Claim-by-claim record

- **§1 The Story of You** — Narrative identity; redemption vs contamination sequences in
  the stories people tell about their lives (**mcadams2013**). Verified.
- **§2 The Two Pillars** — Agency and communion as the two central thematic clusters of
  life narratives (**mcadams2013**). Verified.
- **§3 The Power of Failure** — Pivotal scenes; redemptive interpretation of low points is
  associated with resilience/well-being (**mcadams2013**). Verified.
- **§4 The Advantage of Disadvantage** — Desirable difficulties produce deeper, more
  durable learning (**sb2015**); spacing/distributed practice beats massed cramming
  (**cepeda2006**). Verified. Framed as reframing, not an endorsement of disadvantage.
