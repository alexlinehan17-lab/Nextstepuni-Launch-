# Evidence Dossier — Linking Study to Future Goals

**Module:** `linking-study-future-goals-protocol` (`components/LinkingStudyFutureGoalsModule.tsx`)
**Review date:** 2026-06-23
**Reviewer:** Pre-accreditation literature review (DCU / Brian MacCraith)
**Governing rule:** Only state/advise what peer-reviewed literature supports; every DOI
verified via CrossRef. References surface via inline `Cite` markers + the module-wide
**References** button; data in `data/references/linkingStudyFutureGoals.ts`.

**Reviewer note.** A motivation-science module, and a strong one: each section is built on
a named, canonical theory or intervention with a clean peer-reviewed source, so the work
was mostly attaching the right citation to the right construct. Eight references, all
verified. **No content cut** — only minor phrasing tightening (e.g. "helps close
achievement gaps" → "helped narrow the achievement gap for at-risk students", matching
what the Cohen 2006 study actually reported; "your brain takes it seriously" → "raised
interest and grades", matching Hulleman & Harackiewicz 2009). Two notes for the reviewer:
(i) the self-affirmation / achievement-gap effect (Cohen 2006) is a landmark finding but
has a mixed replication record — the text is hedged ("can build a defence", "helped
narrow") rather than promissory; (ii) **§7 (HEAR / DARE / SUSI)** consists of Irish
admissions/funding administrative facts, which sit *outside* the peer-reviewed-literature
scope of this review — they should be checked against official CAO/HEAR/DARE/SUSI
documentation as a separate factual pass (the "30km" SUSI distance tier and "30–50 point"
HEAR reductions in particular change with policy year).

## Claim-by-claim record

- **§1 The "Why Bother?" Problem** — Motivation as effort weighed against value/cost
  (**wigfield2000**). Verified. Alex's story is testimony; the audit is interactive.
- **§2 The Horizon of Purpose** — Future Time Perspective: a longer, specific FTP reframes
  present tasks as worthwhile (**husman1999**). Verified.
- **§3 The "Writing Cure"** — Utility-value writing raises interest and grades, especially
  for struggling students (**hulleman2009**); brief values-affirmation can buffer
  stereotype threat and narrowed an achievement gap (**cohen2006**, hedged for replication
  nuance). Verified.
- **§4 From Dream to Plan** — Positive fantasising can sap energy (**kappes2011**); mental
  contrasting + specific "if-then" implementation intentions improve follow-through
  (**gollwitzer1999**). Verified.
- **§5 Becoming the Author** — Autonomy as a core motivational need (**ryandeci2000**);
  identity-based motivation and the "difficulty-as-impossibility" interpretation
  (**oyserman2010**). Verified.
- **§6 Curriculum as Career Training** — Argument/framing that subjects build transferable
  skills; the matrix is illustrative; no specific empirical stat, no inline citation.
- **§7 Hacking the System (HEAR/DARE/SUSI)** — Irish admissions/funding administrative
  facts; **outside peer-reviewed scope** — flagged for separate verification against
  official sources (see reviewer note).
- **§8 The Purpose Protocol** — Synthesis/application of the above; no new claim.
