# Evidence Dossier — The Grammar of Grit

**Module:** `grammar-of-grit-protocol` (`components/TheGrammarOfGritModule.tsx`)
**Review date:** 2026-06-23
**Reviewer:** Pre-accreditation literature review (DCU / Brian MacCraith)
**Governing rule:** Only state/advise what peer-reviewed literature supports; every DOI
verified via CrossRef. References surface via inline `Cite` markers + the module-wide
**References** button; data in `data/references/grammarOfGrit.ts`.

**Reviewer note.** Despite the "grit" title, this module is **not** about Duckworth's grit
construct (whose predictive validity is debated) — so that controversy does not apply. It
is about three well-established things: explanatory style and the "3 Ps" of pessimism,
which are the personal/pervasive/permanent restatement of the internal/global/stable
attributions in the reformulated learned-helplessness model (Abramson, Seligman &
Teasdale 1978); cognitive restructuring, a core, well-evidenced CBT technique (Hofmann et
al. 2012 meta-analytic review); and self-compassion with its three components —
self-kindness, common humanity, mindfulness (Neff 2003). **No content cut.** One tiny
tightening: "determines whether you bounce back" → "shapes whether you bounce back."
The Thought Reframer and Self-Talk Diagnostic widgets are interactive exercises.

## Claim-by-claim record

  (**abramson1978**). Verified; "determines" softened to "shapes."
- **§2 The 3 Ps of Failure** — Personal / pervasive / permanent = the internal / global /
  stable attributional dimensions of pessimistic explanatory style (**abramson1978**);
  the optimistic style reverses them. Verified.
- **§3 The Re-Write Protocol** — Cognitive restructuring (catch → question → replace
  automatic thoughts) is a core, well-evidenced CBT technique (**hofmann2012**). Verified.
- **§4 The Role of Self-Compassion** — Self-compassion and its three components —
  self-kindness, common humanity, mindfulness (**neff2003**). Verified.
