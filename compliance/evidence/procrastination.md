# Evidence Dossier — Understanding Procrastination

**Module:** `procrastination-protocol` (`components/ProcrastinationModule.tsx`)
**Review date:** 2026-06-23
**Reviewer:** Pre-accreditation literature review (DCU / Brian MacCraith)
**Governing rule:** Only state/advise what peer-reviewed literature supports; every DOI
verified via CrossRef. References surface via inline `Cite` markers + the module-wide
**References** button; data in `data/references/procrastination.ts`.

**Reviewer note.** A strongly-sourced self-regulation module. Each load-bearing claim
maps to a primary paper: procrastination as short-term mood/emotion regulation rather than
laziness (Sirois & Pychyl 2013); the adolescent limbic-over-prefrontal "amygdala hijack"
framing grounded in adolescent brain development (Casey et al. 2008); the procrastination
equation Motivation = (E×V)/(I×D), which is Steel's Temporal Motivation Theory from his
meta-analytic review (Steel 2007); procrastination as self-handicapping (Berglas & Jones
1978); the headline self-forgiveness finding — students who forgave themselves for
procrastinating before one exam procrastinated less before the next (Wohl, Pychyl &
Bennett 2010); and "if-then" plans as implementation intentions (Gollwitzer 1999).
**No content cut.** The Guilt Divergence / Procrastination Equation / If-Then widgets are
illustrative or practical tools (the divergence chart curves are stylised, not study data).

## Claim-by-claim record

- **§1 The Real Reason You Delay** — Procrastination is short-term mood/emotion regulation,
  not laziness (**siroisPychyl2013**). Verified.
- **§2 The Amygdala Hijack** — The teen prefrontal cortex matures behind the limbic system,
  so emotional reactions can override self-control (**casey2008**); "amygdala hijack" is
  Goleman's popular term for this. Verified.
- **§3 The Procrastination Equation** — Motivation = (Expectancy × Value) / (Impulsiveness ×
  Delay), Steel's Temporal Motivation Theory (**steel2007**). Verified.
- **§4 The Ego's Defence System** — Procrastination as self-handicapping to protect
  self-image (**berglasJones1978**). Verified.
- **§5–6 The Guilt Cycle / Forgiveness Protocol** — Self-criticism feeds avoidance;
  self-forgiveness for past procrastination reduces future procrastination (**wohl2010**).
  Verified.
- **§7 The "If-Then" Protocol** — Implementation intentions automate the start decision
  (**gollwitzer1999**). Verified.
- **§8 Scaffolding Your Focus** — Pomodoro/environment design; practical technique, no
  specific empirical claim, so no inline citation.
