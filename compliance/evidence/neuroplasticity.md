## Claim-by-claim record

- **§1 The Plastic Brain** — Experience/training physically reshapes brain structure
  (**draganski2004**); adolescence is a period of heightened plasticity and reorganisation
  (**casey2008**). Verified.
- **§2 The Brain's Renovation** — Synaptic pruning ("use it or lose it") in adolescence
  (**casey2008**); training thickens/strengthens white-matter (myelinated) pathways,
  speeding signalling (**scholz2009**). Verified.
- **§3 The Rulebook** — "Cells that fire together, wire together" — activity-dependent
  synaptic strengthening, whose physiological basis is long-term potentiation
  (**bliss1973**). Verified. The Desire-Path simulation is illustrative.
- **§4 The Physical Evidence** — The juggling study: 3 months' training grew the relevant
  grey-matter regions; stopping reversed it (**draganski2004**). Verified.
- **§5 The Learning Blueprint** — Spacing (**cepeda2006**) and retrieval practice
  (**rk2006**) are the techniques that best drive durable change. Verified.
- **§6 System Maintenance** — Sleep consolidates and prunes (**diekelmann2010**); chronic
  stress / elevated cortisol impairs plasticity and can harm existing circuits, especially
  in memory regions (**lupien2009**). Verified.
