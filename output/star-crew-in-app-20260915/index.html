Recommended placements & implementation notes

Personal crew

The original eight belong in account creation and profile settings. Use the chosen character at 40–48px in account controls and at 112px in the profile sheet. Keep the name beside it.

Subject crew

Use one consistent character beside each subject: 44px in plan rows, 48–52px in onboarding, and 64px in the larger study cards. Keep subject labels visible.

A little room to focus

Grade goals use subject names and grade controls. Home and study retain their existing layouts; artwork goes in the existing subject rows and the larger study cards.

Fit the current app

The home section and study screen below use the current app components. Enlarge the study subject cards and replace their dots with character artwork. Preserve the setup sequence.
