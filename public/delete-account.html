Delete your NextStepUni account

NextStepUni · NEXTSTEPUNI LIMITED

You can delete your NextStepUni account, and everything stored with it, from inside the app at any time. You do not need to contact us, and no one needs to approve it.

How to delete your account

  1. Open NextStepUni and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar at the top of the screen, then Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account and read the warning, then Continue.
  5. Confirm it is you — your password, or Apple or Google if that is how you signed up.
  6. Type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you would like a copy of your information first, choose Download my data on the same screen before deleting.

Cannot get into the app? Email nextstepuniinfo@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will erase it for you.

What is deleted

All of the following are erased permanently:

  • Your sign-in account, so you can no longer log in
  • Your profile — name, email address, school and year group
  • Your study progress, study sessions and points
  • Your spaced-repetition history in Mark Bank
  • Your settings and preferences
  • Your written reflections and answers to module questions
  • Your notifications
  • Your public profile shown to classmates
  • Kudos and gifts you have sent or received
  • Any flags or cohort tags a guidance counsellor has recorded about you

What is kept, and for how long

One record is retained: that an erasure was requested and carried out. It holds an internal account identifier, the date and time, and a count of how many items were removed. It does not contain your name, your email address, or anything you wrote in the app.

We keep it because data protection law requires us to be able to show that we honoured your request. It is never used for any other purpose. Everything else listed above is gone.

Our full retention practices are described in our Privacy Notice.
