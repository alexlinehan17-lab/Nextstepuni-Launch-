## Decisions taken

- **Card unit = the scheme's mark cell.** One cell over several parts -> one
  orderedSplit card with null rows; separate cells -> separate cards; a
  choice ("[any two]") -> one anyN row; A/B alternatives each their own
  card(s).
- **Re-carding a pre-audit card** (`chem_apply_print.py`): an id is never
  dropped silently. A card that joined parts with cells of their own keeps
  its id on its FIRST part (concept reassigned if the old slug named the
  joined parts); later parts get new ids. A duplicate, or a piece of a task
  one cell prices, is listed in the generator's `WITHDRAWN` and is dropped
  at build with an alias. The ledger
  `scripts/markbank/authored/chemistry-audit-ledger.json` records every id
  as added / corrected / unchanged / withdrawn (with before/after hashes and
  whether the base build shipped it).
- **Preservation (test/markBankCardPreservation.test.ts)** is additive for
  Chemistry: the deck entries keep the ORIGINAL frozen counts and hashes
  (496 Higher `ef4c5a89…`, 380 Ordinary `f15304f5…`); the check rebuilds the
  frozen set from the live deck less ledger "added" ids plus shipped
  withdrawals. The audit test pins per-paper counts and checks every
  withdrawal aliases a live card of the same year and level. Bank total
  19,570; alias count 56; identity total 10,012.
- **Drawing answers** (coordinator-approved): carded; the row is the paper's
  ask, the answer is the scheme's drawing as a solution crop held back until
  the reveal (`Cards.drawn`). Where the part also needs the paper's picture,
  the question figure is a stacked paper crop (`questionFigureKey`, as
  2026 OL Q8(a)(iii)/(iv)).
- **The seven 2026 OL open parts**: Q8(a)(iii)/(iv) are now carded; the
  other five are recorded exclusions (above).
- **Figures as pictures**: `Printed(year, lvl, figures=True)` removes text
  lying inside a sitting's paper crops (or `'labels'` regions) from asks and
  lead-ins, so diagram labels never reach a stem. Used from 2025 on.
- **Fractions** the text layer cannot order are given in the one form the
  provenance gate finds ("0.010 ÷ 1000 × 22.5 = …") with a note, or shown
  as a scheme crop. A fix that no longer matches the reader's text is now
  refused (`Cards._fixes_applied`).
- QuestionText "(B)"/"(ii)" mis-split in the session screen: left for the
  shared UI fix (cards named in the status file).
