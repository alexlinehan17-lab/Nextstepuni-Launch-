## Research Study Report 2025 — descriptor grids

**Section A — Rationale & Approach (10 marks):** Clear engagement 6–10 / Engagement is lacking 0–5.
Looks for: outline of project aim; link with prescribed topic; overview of research approach and/or
sources. **All three points not required for full marks. NB: 10 marks, requires specific points
related to project.** Lower band: attempt made to outline aim, link with prescribed topic, or gives
an overview of research approach/sources.

**Section B — Extended Essay — Knowledge/Understanding (20 marks):** Thorough 16–20 / Basic 9–15 /
Very Basic 0–8.
- Thorough: Summary of accurate knowledge that is in-depth or wide ranging, but at times lacks
  relevance to chosen topic. Demonstrates a very good understanding of the chosen topic.
- Basic: Includes knowledge that indicates attempts at research, but has inconsistencies in accuracy
  or relevance. Demonstrates a good understanding at times.
- Very Basic: Contains only generic or superficial information. Does not convey an overall
  understanding of the chosen topic.

**Section B — Extended Essay — Analysis/Critical thinking (20 marks):** Thorough 16–20 / Basic 9–15
/ Very Basic 0–8.
- Thorough: Analysis is logically argued with developed points; good to very good critical thinking.
- Basic: Analysis is generic and lacks development; critical thinking at times.
- Very Basic: Attempts at analysis, mostly unsuccessful; does not engage with analysis/critical
  thinking.

**Section B — Extended Essay — Evidence/Research (20 marks):** Thorough 16–20 / Basic 9–15 / Very
Basic 0–8.
- Thorough: Sources included as part of discussion / development of points, where relevant.
- Basic: Sources referred to in essay, where appropriate.
- Very Basic: Sources included only in bibliography.

<!-- page 5 -->

**Section C — Review & Reflection (10 marks):** Clear engagement 6–10 / Engagement is lacking 0–5.
Looks for: assessment of project; reflection on significance of research/project; self-reflection on
research/writing process. **All three points not required for full marks. NB: 10 marks, requires
specific points related to project.** Lower band: attempt made to review research/writing process or
reflect on learning.

**Overall Coherence — Communication & Structure (20 marks):** Mostly coherent 16–20 / Lacking
coherence 9–15 / Incoherent 0–8. Rewards overarching structure, easy-to-follow language, RSR aim
achieved, satisfies criteria in the 2025 RSR Brief. Incoherent band: poorly presented and lacking
structure to the extent that the aim of the RSR is not communicated.

To be noted by examiner:
- Read everything in the RSR before marking anything.
- Be careful not to penalise skilful brevity, nor to reward unwarranted length.

Marking steps: 1) Read the full RSR. 2) Re-read and mark each section: Section A, Section B, Section
C and Overall Coherence.

## Section B: 100 marks

### Question 11: 50 marks

- **(a) 20 marks:** 5 marks for any valid lines + 15 marks for explanation, banded (based on level of
  understanding): 15 partial/full; 10 basic/incomplete; 5 very basic.

- **(b) 30 marks.** Each of the questions 11bi, 11bii, 11biii has the same marking scheme, divided
  into two components:
  - Development of material required to fully answer the question (20 marks)
  - Overall quality of the answer (10 marks)

  **Development of Material (20 marks):**
  - 10 marks for each unit of development.
  - Features of a unit of development: candidate makes relevant point, includes relevant evidence to
    support the point (knowledge/understanding) and makes **some development** of the point
    (analysis/elaboration/discussion).
  - Each unit marked on level of development: **10 marks for a developed point; 7 marks for a basic
    point; 4 marks for a very basic point.**

  **Overall Quality (10 marks):** overall engagement with question and understanding of material.
  - **8–10 marks: Good Quality** — Focuses on the question; points are structured and supported by
    relevant and accurate evidence.
  - **1–7 marks: Low Quality** — Limited engagement with the question; attempt to make points, but
    lack of evidence/examples to support points; relies mostly on narrative.

### Questions 12–16: 50 marks

Each has the same marking scheme, divided into two components:
- Development of material required to fully answer the question (30 marks)
- Overall quality of the answer (20 marks)

**Development of Material (30 marks):**
- Features of a unit of development: candidate makes relevant point, includes relevant evidence to
  support the point (knowledge/understanding) and makes **some development** of the point
  (analysis/elaboration/discussion).
- **Indicative structure of units: (15 + 15), (10+10+10), (8+8+7+7).**
- Each unit marked on level of development: **11–15 marks for a well-developed point; 8–10 marks for
  a developed point; 1–7 marks for a basic point.**

**Overall Quality (20 marks):** overall engagement with question and understanding of material.
- **15–20 marks: Good Quality** — Focuses on the question; points are structured and supported by
  relevant and accurate evidence.
- **1–14 marks: Low Quality** — Limited engagement with the question; attempt to make points, but
  lack of evidence/examples to support points; relies mostly on narrative.
