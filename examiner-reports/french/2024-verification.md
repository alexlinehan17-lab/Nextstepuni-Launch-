## Summary

**All five rules CONFIRMED for 2024 HL. No drift, no CHANGED, no NOT-PRESENT.**
The 2024 scheme's wording is materially identical to the 2025 scheme on every
cited rule — same two-axis Communication+Language structure and mark caps, the
same verbatim "Mere transcription or very poor treatment of stimulus material"
Bottom-band descriptor, the same manipulation-required / direct-quotation-
acceptable tagging with the same −1 docking of a raw lift where manipulation is
required, the same "If more than one answer offered = 0 Marks" hedging rule, and
the same "accept any formulation" + whole-section-in-French −50% aural rules.

**Change needing a session edit:** none — the five cited rules hold across two
independent years (2024 and 2025).

**Unverified / caveats:** none material. Source is an educateplus.ie mirror of
the SEC PDF (same provenance route as the filed 2025 scheme); cover page and
internal headers confirm it is the official 2024 SEC Higher Level scheme, not a
mock. The internal header on p.3 labels it "Draft Marking Scheme" — this is the
SEC's standard designation on published schemes and does not indicate a
non-final document.
