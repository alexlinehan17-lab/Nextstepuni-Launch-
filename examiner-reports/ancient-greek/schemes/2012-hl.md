(i)  Oligarchical Revolution; Alkibiades; Persian intervention; Spartan fleet; naval

Killed in Egyptian expedition 361 BC.   Life by Xenophon

Four times.  Killed at Mantinea 362 BC.  Military tactic of phalanx.
