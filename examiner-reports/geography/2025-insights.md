- **Year:** 2025
- **Level:** Higher

## The SRP system — definition and value (p.3)

The scheme's own definition (verbatim, p.3):

> "For the purpose of marking a Significant Relevant Point (SRP) is a single piece of factual
> information, to which an examiner will assign a mark weighting as prescribed in the marking
> scheme (typically 2 marks). As previously advised in Circular S85/09 and in the Sample Marking
> Indicators available on the SEC website each SRP should emerge from the information put
> forward by the candidate, leading to an overall coherent response to the question."

General instructions to examiners (p.3):

- Detail required "is determined by the context and the manner in which the question is asked
  and by the number of marks assigned to the answer".
- "Words, expressions or phrases must be correctly used in context and not contradicted, and
  where there is evidence of incorrect use or contradiction, the marks may not be awarded."
- "if in doubt about the validity of any answer, examiners must consult their advising examiner".
- The scheme's suggestions "are not exhaustive and alternative valid answers etc. are acceptable".
- Standard annotation palette (p.3): valid information stamps worth 1–4 marks, "Surplus
  information" (no marks), "Incorrect answer 0", "Significant part of the answer", "Page seen by
  examiner / Information not valid".

## Paper structure and mark allocation

- **Part One — Short-Answer Questions, 80 marks (p.4):** 12 questions; "Any 10 questions @ 8
  marks each. Mark all questions attempted and award questions with highest mark. No grading."
  Each 8-mark question splits into small items worth 1 or 2 marks each (mostly 4 × 2 marks).
- **Part Two — structured questions, 80 marks per question:** Section 1 Physical (Q1–3, p.5),
  Section 2 Regional (Q4–6, p.15), Section 3 Electives — Economic Q7–9 / Human Q10–12 (p.24).
  Each question = part A (20 marks, skills/stimulus) + parts B and C (30 marks each).
- **Section 4 — Options, Q13–24, 80 marks each (p.46):** essay-style, marked with the aspects +
  Overall Coherence grid (below).
- **Geographical Investigation** coursework: 100 marks, sections 5/5/40/30/20 (pp.61–66).
- **Bonus for answering in Irish** (Appendix 2, p.68): normal-rate bonus (10% of a 400-mark
  total) up to 75% of total marks; above 75% a tapering table applies. "Geographical
  Investigation – Not included in total for bonus marks."

## The 30-mark part grammar (parts B and C throughout)

Every 30-mark part is decomposed into **named/identified elements @ 2 marks each, plus a
prescribed count of SRPs (each worth 2 marks)** that together sum to 30. Representative
allocations:

- "Landform named 2 marks / Examination 14 x SRPs" (Q2B(i), p.9).
- "Rock 1 and associated landscape named 2 + 2 marks / Rock 2 ... 2 + 2 marks / Examination
  11 x SRPs" (Q1B, p.6).
- Two-heading answers split the SRP pool: "Explanation of physical weathering 7/6 x SRPs /
  Explanation of chemical weathering 6/7 x SRPs" (Q2C, p.11); likewise 7/6–6/7 for
  social/economic EU challenges (Q4C, p.17) and factor 1 / factor 2 splits (Q4B, p.16).
- Naming credit is itself SRP-convertible: "Credit 1 x SRP for a second impact/feature
  identified from description/explanation. All further impacts/features require
  description/explanation" (Q1C, p.7) — i.e. lists of extra named items without development
  stop earning after one.

## Diagram and sketch-map credit rules (recur on pp.6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 16)

Standard formula, repeated per question:

- "Credit 1 x SRP for a relevant labelled diagram from examination. Diagram without labelling
  0 marks." (e.g. Q1B, p.6)
- "Credit 2 x SRPs for additional relevant information on a labelled diagram ... This must be
  information not already awarded in the written account." (e.g. Q1B, p.6) — no double credit
  for repeating the prose in the diagram.
- Regional variant: "Credit 1 x SRP for a valid labelled sketch map from examination" + 1 SRP
  for additional information on it (Q4B, p.16).

## Penalty caps and zero rules ("Max n × SRPs")

- **Description without the demanded relationship is capped hard**: "Max 2 x SRPs if there is
  merely a description of folding/faulting without a reference to landform development"
  (Q3C, p.14); same 2-SRP cap for description of isostasy without Irish-landscape impact
  (Q1C, p.7), landform without formation (Q2B(i), p.9), mass movement without factors
  (Q2B(ii), p.10). Per-factor versions: "Max 1 x SRP per factor if there is merely a
  description of the factor without a reference to the development of primary activity"
  (Q4B, p.16); "Max 4 x SRPs per factor" (Q7B, p.26).
- **Answering half the question halves the ceiling**: "Max 6 x SRPs if there is only one rock
  type and landscape examined" (Q1B, p.6); "If only physical weathering process or chemical
  weathering process explained max 7 x SRPs" (Q2C, p.11); "Max 7 x SRPs if there is only a
  social challenge(s) explained or only an economic challenge(s) explained" (Q4C, p.17).
- **Missing the anchor scores zero**: "Examination without link to a named or clearly inferred
  region 0 marks" (Q4B, p.16); "Discussion without link to named multinational company 0 marks"
  (Q7B, p.26); wrong process entirely: "Examination of processes of erosion 0 marks" on a
  deposition question (Q2B(i), p.9).
- **Multiple attempts marked separately, best credited**: "Where more than one developed economy
  is examined mark separately and credit the highest mark" (Q9B, p.33).

## OS sketch-map marking (part A skills questions, 20 marks; pp.5 and 25)

- Allocation: "Sketch Outline 4 marks (4/0); 4 features @ 4 marks each — Shown 3 marks (graded
  3/1/0), Label 1 mark" (p.5).
- Outline must be **half scale, 12 cm × 9 cm (±0.5 cm), landscape orientation**; "The sketch
  must have four sides drawn. Using the edge of the page is not acceptable. Lose 4 marks for
  sketch outline" (p.5).
- "If sketch is traced or only a section of the map is drawn, lose 4 marks for sketch outline
  and lose 3 marks for showing per item. Allow labelling marks only" (p.5).
- "If the aerial photograph is used instead of the Ordnance Survey map 0 marks" (p.5).
- Labels may be on the sketch or in a key; unlabelled features get 0 showing marks; a feature
  located incorrectly but labelled correctly keeps its labelling mark (p.5).
- "Reasonable accuracy (location and proportion) for full showing marks" (p.5).
- Ireland outline-map variant (Q4A, p.15): "Map outline 4 marks graded (4/2/0); 4 features @ 4
  marks each — Shown 2 marks graded (2/1/0), Named 2 marks"; naming marks are creditable even
  if the feature is mis-located, but "Where features are shown but named incorrectly or not
  named at all, then 0 marks for showing and 0 marks for naming" (p.15).

## Graph-drawing marking (Q9A, 16 marks, p.31; same grid at Q12A, p.42)

- Bar chart: Title 2; vertical axis named 1; horizontal axis named 1; 6 items @ 2 marks each
  graded 2/1/0. Pie chart: Title 2; circle & centred 1 + 1; 6 sectors @ 2 graded.
- "Naming of graph/chart type not sufficient for title mark. The title must have reference to
  chart content" (p.31).
- "If graph paper is not used deduct 2 marks from total" (p.31).
- The 2/1/0 grading is applied "if the numbered spaces and the column widths are not plotted
  accurately" (p.31).

## Options essays (Q13–24, 80 marks) — aspects + Overall Coherence (pp.46–48)

- Paper rubric (p.46): "It is better to discuss three or four aspects of the theme in some
  detail, rather than to give a superficial treatment of a large number of points."
- Outline scheme (p.47) — examiner selects the column matching the number of aspects discussed:

  | | 3 aspects @ 20 marks | 4 aspects @ 15 marks |
  |---|---|---|
  | Identifying aspect | 4 marks | 3 marks |
  | Discussion | 8 x SRPs | 6 x SRPs |
  | Overall Coherence | 20 marks graded | 20 marks graded |

- **Overall Coherence descriptors (p.47)** — "the examiner will consider how well the candidate
  deals with the set question": Excellent 20 ("Excellent ability to relate knowledge to the set
  question"), Very Good 17, Good 14, Fair 10 ("Some relevant information presented but
  insufficient application of information to set question"), Weak 6 ("Very limited engagement
  with set question"), Poor 0 ("Failure to address the question resulting in a largely
  irrelevant answer").
- Per-question caps (e.g. Q13, p.49; Q14, p.50; Q15, p.51): "Allow for up to 3 examples to a
  max of 3 x SRPs (different examples and in different aspects)"; "Allow for up to 2 labelled
  illustrations to a max of 2 x SRPs (different illustrations and in different aspects)".

## Geographical Investigation marking (100 marks, pp.61–66)

- Section allocations: Introduction 5 (4 × SRPs @ 1 mark + Coherence/Legibility 1, p.62);
  Planning 5 (same grid, p.62); Gathering of Data 40 ("Two methods / tasks @ 18 marks each. For
  each method / task: 9 x SRPs @ 2 marks each; C/L: 4 marks graded (4/2/0)", p.63); Results,
  Conclusions, Evaluation 30 ("Three headings @ 8 marks each. For each heading: 4 x SRPs @ 2
  marks each; C/L: 6 marks graded (6/4/2/0)", p.64); Organisation & Presentation of Results 20
  (two different presentation methods, 4 × SRPs @ 2 each; Overall Coherence 4 graded 4/2/0,
  p.65).
- Excessive-material rule (p.61): "Where excessive material is added to a section of the GI,
  allow the material for SRPs where relevant but award 0 marks for OC in that section."
- Gathering section discipline (p.63): "Simple statements are not sufficient e.g. I observed
  the features, I recorded the results ... There must be some qualification of the statement
  e.g. how/where?"; "No marks for results in this written section"; "Work completed solely by
  the teacher = 0 marks" (Planning, p.62).
- Results/Conclusions/Evaluations "must be tied to aims and tied to activities carried out in
  the gathering of information section"; "Conclusions are based directly or indirectly on the
  results, otherwise it is evaluation"; "No double marking" across sections (p.64).
- Presentation: "There must be two different forms of presentation"; OC "Cannot be awarded ...
  if only one method of presentation is evident" (p.65); per-format mark breakdowns (title,
  axis, plotted items etc., each 1 × SRP = 2 marks) on p.66.
