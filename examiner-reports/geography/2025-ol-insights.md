## Marking-grammar STRUCTURE (Ordinary Level) — and how it differs from HL

### 1. The SRP is worth 3 marks at OL, not 2 (p.3) — the single biggest difference

The page-3 definition is identical to HL except for the weighting:

> "For the purpose of marking a Significant Relevant Point (SRP) is a single piece of factual
> information, to which an examiner will assign a mark weighting as prescribed in the marking
> scheme (**typically 3 marks**)." (p.3)

At HL the same sentence reads "typically 2 marks" (HL scheme p.3). So across the OL **written
paper** every developed point is worth 3 marks, and the "n × SRPs" counts are correspondingly
smaller for the same mark total (e.g. a 30-mark heading = **10 SRPs @ 3m** at OL vs 14–15 SRPs
@ 2m at HL). **Exception:** the Geographical Investigation keeps the HL-style 1m/2m SRP weightings
(see §6) — the 3m SRP is a written-paper feature only.

### 2. There is NO Options section and NO Overall Coherence essay grid at OL

The OL paper has only **three parts**: the Geographical Investigation (Core Unit 3, pp.4–5),
Part One Short-Answer (pp.6–7), and Part Two Structured questions (Sections 1–3, Qs 1–12,
pp.8–34). It **stops at Question 12** (p.34) — there is no Section 4 Options (HL Qs 13–24), and
therefore **no essay-length answers and no Overall Coherence grading grid** (the HL 3-aspect /
4-aspect Excellent-20…Poor-0 grid on HL p.47 has no OL counterpart). At OL the longest single
answer is a 40-mark part built from named elements + a fixed SRP count; nothing is graded holistically
on "how well the candidate deals with the set question". Coherence at OL appears **only inside the
Geographical Investigation** (§6), not on the written paper.

### 3. Part One — Short-Answer: 100 marks, any 10 @ 10 marks (pp.6–7)

- "ANY TEN QUESTIONS @ 10 MARKS EACH. Mark all questions attempted and award ten questions with
  highest marks." (p.6) — 12 questions offered, best 10 count, no grading.
- **HL difference:** HL Part One is **80 marks, any 10 @ 8 marks** (HL p.4). OL is 100 marks /
  10-mark questions.
- Each 10-mark OL question decomposes into small items: usually **5 × 2m** (matching/true-false/one-word
  recall — e.g. Q1 five letters @ 2m, Q7 five true/false @ 2m, p.7), or **2 × 5m** for map-skills
  (Q5 "North east 5m / 7 km² 5m", p.6). Answers are one-word / letter / figure — pure recall.

### 4. Part Two — Structured: 300 marks, THREE questions @ 100 marks, part shape A-30 / B-40 / C-30

- "You must attempt THREE questions … one question from two different sections … The third question
  can be from any section. All questions carry equal marks." (p.8) Sections: 1 Physical (Q1–3),
  2 Regional (Q4–6), 3 Electives (Q7–12).
- **Each question = 100 marks**, split **Part A 30m + Part B 40m + Part C 30m**.
- **HL difference:** HL Part Two questions are **80 marks each** (A 20 + B 30 + C 30, HL p.5). OL
  parts are larger (A 30 / B 40 / C 30 = 100), and OL requires **three** structured questions where
  HL requires two structured + the Options essay.

### 5. Part A (data-response / map skills) — heavy recall, 30 marks

Two OL flavours of Part A:

- **Sketch-map from OS map / aerial photo** (Q1A p.9, Q7A p.23, Q8B map p.25, Q10A p.29):
  - Sketch Outline = **Limits/Frame 1m + Proportions (landscape) 2m + Overall Impression 2m graded
    (2/1/0) = 5m**.
  - **Five items** shown and labelled: **Showing 3m graded (3/1/0) + Labelled 2m = 5m each → 25m**.
    Total 30m.
  - Tracing penalty (p.9, p.23): "For an obvious tracing allow 50% of the marks obtained for showing
    and labelling items and 0m out of 5m for limits, proportion and overall impression."
  - **HL difference:** HL sketch is **4 features** with "Sketch Outline 4 marks (4/0); 4 features @
    4 marks — Shown 3 (3/1/0), Label 1" and a strict half-scale 12×9 cm outline rule (HL p.5). OL
    asks for **five** items, grades showing 3/1/0 with a separate 2-mark label, and the outline is
    scored on limits/proportion/impression rather than a measured 12×9 cm frame.
- **Data-response stimulus** (Q2A p.11, Q5A p.18, Q6A p.19, Q8A p.24, Q9A p.27, Q11A p.31): four
  or five sub-parts, mostly **single-answer @ 6m** (e.g. "Pacific 6m", "84 (%) 6m", p.12/p.20), a
  "list/state two … 3m+3m", and one closing **"Brief explanation 2 SRP's @ 3m each = 6m"**. The
  6-marks-for-one-correct-word tariff is an OL hallmark with no HL equivalent (HL Part A is 20 marks
  of skills work, not recall).
- **Graph-drawing** (Q9A p.27, Q12A p.33): "Graph paper 3m; Vertical axis labelled/Circle 3m graded
  (3/1/0); Horizontal axis labelled/Centred 3m graded (3/1/0); 5 items @ 3m each graded (3/1/0) =
  15m → Sub-total 24m", + brief-explanation 6m = 30m.
  - **HL difference:** HL graph (Q9A HL p.31) is **16 marks** with a **Title 2m** mark, axes @ 1m,
    **6 items @ 2m**, and "if graph paper is not used deduct 2 marks". At OL there is **no title
    mark**, graph paper is worth **3m in its own right**, and only **5 items @ 3m** are plotted.

### 6. Geographical Investigation — 100 marks, SRPs stay at 1m/2m (pp.4–5)

- Sections: **1. Introduction 5m** (4 SRPs @ 1m + Conformity with prescribed length 1m);
  **2. Planning 5m** (same grid); **3. Gathering of Data 40m** (two methods/tasks @ 18m — each
  **9 SRPs @ 2m = 36m** + Coherence 2m graded (2/1/0) + Conformity 2m); **4. Results, Conclusions,
  Evaluation 30m** (three headings @ 8m — each **4 SRPs @ 2m = 24m** + Coherence 4m graded (4/2/0) +
  Conformity 2m); **5. Organisation and Presentation of Results 20m** (two different methods, each
  **5 SRPs @ 2m**).
- "Allow one SRP for diagram for each Method/Task." (Gathering, p.4)
- Presentation breakdown (p.5): per chart — axes/circle 2m each, data 2m + 2m, Overall impression
  2m graded (2/1/0); per diagram/sketch/map — annotated info Shown 2m + Named 2m (×2) + impression;
  per table — title/rows/columns/data @ 2m + impression.
- **HL difference:** OL GI SRPs are the **same 1m/2m weightings as HL GI** (the 3m written-paper SRP
  does not apply here). OL labels the graded quality mark **"Coherence"** and adds an explicit
  **"Conformity with prescribed length"** mark; the HL GI used a combined **"Coherence/Legibility
  (C/L)"** mark instead (HL pp.62–65). Section totals (5/5/40/30/20) are identical across levels.

### 7. Diagram credit rule at OL

- Standard formula, repeated per B/C part: **"Allow one SRP for relevant annotated diagram"** (= 3m
  at OL) and **"Relevant information, additional to the text, given on diagrams can be awarded
  SRP's."** (e.g. Q1C p.11, Q2B p.13, Q3B p.15, Q3C p.15). As at HL, diagram credit is **not
  double-counted** with prose ("additional to the text").
- **Landform-formation exception (Q1B(i), p.10):** here diagrams get an explicit tariff —
  "Diagram(s) two aspects @ 2m each = 4m", *plus* "Formation explained 5 SRP's @ 3m each = 15m",
  with "At least one SRP must explain a relevant process." So a landform answer = named 1m + diagram
  4m + 5 SRPs @3m = 20m per landform, two landforms = 40m.
- **HL difference:** HL phrases it "Credit 1 x SRP for a relevant labelled diagram … Diagram without
  labelling 0 marks" and "Credit 2 x SRPs for additional relevant information" (HL p.6). Same logic;
  OL just prices the SRP at 3m and, uniquely, hands out a flat 4-mark diagram allocation on the
  landform question.

### 8. SRP-conversion and "at least one SRP for explanation" discipline (all @ 3m)

- Naming/identifying credit is convertible to one SRP but **stops after one**: "Allow a second
  interaction identified for one SRP. All further interactions must be explained for SRP's."
  (Q2C, p.13); "Allow a second impact identified for one SRP" (Q9C, p.28); "Allow a named region /
  named example / named policy for one SRP" recurs throughout (pp.11, 16, 17, 20, 28).
- Recurrent floor: **"At least one SRP for explanation"** (Q1B, Q1C, Q2B, Q2C, Q3B, Q3C, and most
  C-parts) — pure description without any explanation cannot take the full mark. This is the OL
  analogue of HL's harder "Max n × SRPs if merely a description …" caps, but stated as a minimum
  rather than a numeric ceiling: OL does **not** use the HL "Max 2 x SRPs / Max 6 x SRPs" penalty
  caps at all — the OL scheme relies on "at least one SRP for explanation" instead.
- Two-heading answers split the SRP pool, e.g. Q5B "Activity 1 explained 7 SRP's @ 3m / Activity 2
  explained 6 SRP's @ 3m / Region named 1m" (p.19); Q3B two weathering types **6 SRPs @ 3m each**
  (p.15); Q10B migration positive 6 SRPs / negative 6 SRPs @ 3m (p.30).

## Quotable lines (with page refs)

1. **The OL SRP tariff (p.3):**
   > "a Significant Relevant Point (SRP) is a single piece of factual information, to which an
   > examiner will assign a mark weighting as prescribed in the marking scheme (typically 3 marks)."
   (Contrast HL: "typically 2 marks".)
2. **Description alone is not enough — the OL explanation floor (Q1C, p.11; recurring):**
   > "At least one SRP for explanation."
3. **Naming credit is capped at one point (Q2C, p.13):**
   > "Allow a second interaction identified for one SRP. All further interactions must be explained
   > for SRP's."
4. **Diagram marks are for new information only (Q2B, p.13):**
   > "Relevant information, additional to the text, given on diagrams can be awarded SRP's."
5. **Tracing wipes the map-construction marks (Q1A, p.9):**
   > "For an obvious tracing allow 50% of the marks obtained for showing and labelling items and 0m
   > out of 5m for limits, proportion and overall impression."

## OL-specific candidate-behaviour findings (source + page)

1. **At OL, a single correct word earns 6 marks in Part A data-response questions** (e.g. "Pacific
   6m", Q2A(i) p.12; "84 (%) 6m", Q6A(i) p.20) — the front-loaded recall marks make the stimulus
   parts the cheapest marks on the paper, so leaving any blank is disproportionately costly.
2. **Every developed point at OL is worth 3 marks, so a 30-mark heading needs only ~10 points**
   (Q4C "Description/explanation 10 SRP's @ 3m each", p.17) — students who over-write past the
   required SRP count gain nothing, and the scheme awards no coherence/essay bonus to reward volume
   (no Overall Coherence grid exists at OL — pp.8–34).
3. **The recurring "brief explanation 2 SRP's @ 3m each = 6m"** closer on data-response questions
   (Q2A(v) p.12, Q5A(v) p.18, Q9A(ii) p.27) rewards two short developed sentences — candidates who
   give only a one-line statement forfeit the second 3-mark SRP.
4. **Sketch maps require five clearly-shown-and-labelled items**, with showing graded 3/1/0 and
   labelling a separate flat 2m (Q1A p.9, Q10A p.29); an item shown but unlabelled loses the 2 label
   marks, and tracing forfeits all 5 outline marks (p.9). Accuracy/proportion, not artistry, drives
   the showing mark.
5. **Description without any explanation is self-capping at OL** via "At least one SRP for
   explanation" on essentially every B/C part (pp.11–34) — a purely descriptive answer cannot reach
   full marks even though OL uses no explicit "Max n × SRPs" penalty caps (the HL cap mechanism, HL
   pp.6–17, is absent from the OL scheme).
