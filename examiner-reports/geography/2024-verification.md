# Geography HL — Second-Year SRP-Rule Verification (2024 vs app claims)

## Purpose

Depth-item cross-check for LC Geography, Higher Level. The app teaches five SRP /
marking rules sourced from the **2025** HL marking scheme
(`2025-marking-scheme.md`). This document re-verifies each rule against a
**different** year — the **2024** HL marking scheme
(`2024-marking-scheme.pdf` / `.md`) — to confirm the rules are stable across
sittings, not artefacts of a single year.

## Source verified against

- **State Examinations Commission — Leaving Certificate 2024 Marking Scheme,
  Higher Level, Geography** (64-page PDF).
- Mirror: educateplus.ie (`Geography HL.pdf`), saved as
  `examiner-reports/geography/2024-marking-scheme.pdf`.
- Cover page ("Leaving Certificate 2024 … Marking Scheme … Higher Level …
  Geography") confirms year and level — page 1 of the extracted MD.
- Official SEC document (not a mock / Pre-Leaving): note-to-teachers boilerplate
  on page 2, SEC branding, Circular S85/09 reference on page 3.

Page references below are the **printed PDF page numbers** ("Page X of 60") where
the text carries them; the MD line numbers are in parentheses for locating the
text in `2024-marking-scheme.md`.

## Detail per rule

### Rule 1 — SRP definition — CONFIRMED

> "For the purpose of marking a Significant Relevant Point (SRP) is a single
> piece of factual information, to which an examiner will assign a mark weighting
> as prescribed in the marking scheme (**typically 2 marks**)."
> — Printed Page 3 of 60 (MD lines 68–69).

Identical wording and "typically 2 marks" weighting to the 2025 scheme. Confirmed.

### Rule 2 — Diagram / labelling — CONFIRMED

The pairing "Credit relevant labelled diagram for 1 x SRP. **Diagram without
labelling 0 marks.**" recurs throughout Part Two physical/regional answers —
e.g. MD lines 247, 280, 334, 363, 402, 462, 492 (Section 1 & 2 questions,
printed pp.9–15). Additional labelled-diagram information is worth 2 x SRP's,
matching 2025. Confirmed verbatim.

### Rule 3 — Wrong-process scores 0 — CONFIRMED (mirror-direction example)

The app's example is phrased "Examination of processes of erosion 0 marks"
(where **deposition** was required). The 2024 paper set the mirror-image
question (erosion required), and the scheme states the equivalent rule:

> "Focus of question is on the role of the processes of **erosion** on the
> formation of one landform. **Explanation of processes of deposition 0
> marks.**" — Printed Page 10 of 60 (MD line 333).

Same principle — answering the wrong process (deposition when erosion is
required, or vice-versa) scores 0. The 2024 scheme also carries the broader
family of "wrong-focus 0" rules (e.g. "Examination without link to a named …
region 0 marks", MD lines 593, 663, 695, 759). Confirmed.

### Rule 4 — Overall Coherence graded /20 — CONFIRMED (exact bands)

Options (Section 4, Questions 13–24) essays carry an "Overall Coherence 20 marks
graded*" line in every question's outline (MD lines 1458, 1516, 1540, 1596,
1652, 1686, 1713, 1733, etc.). The graded descriptor table lists the exact band
values the app teaches:

> "The following are used when marking the Overall Coherence:
> Excellent **20** / Very Good **17** / Good **14** / Fair **10** / Weak **6** /
> Poor **0**." — Printed Page 46 of 60 (MD lines 1500–1506; descriptor prose
> begins MD line 1463, printed p.45).

All six band values match the app exactly. Confirmed.

### Rule 5 — Part Two = 80 marks = A(20)+B(30)+C(30) — CONFIRMED

> "All questions carry **80 marks**." — Printed Page 7 of 60 (MD line 230),
> repeated at MD lines 547, 816, 1441.

Each Part Two question is structured as three parts: A **[20m]**, B **[30m]**,
C **[30m]** (20+30+30 = 80). Example — Question 1 (Section 1, Physical
Environment): part A at MD line 203 **[20m]**, part B at MD line 239 **[30m]**,
part C at MD line 274 **[30m]**. Confirmed.

Note: the 2024 paper introduced **additional candidate choice** in Part Two —
candidates attempt FOUR questions including one from three of the four sections,
with the fourth from any section (printed Page 5 of 60, MD lines 149–157). This
is a paper-structure/choice change, not a change to the per-question
A(20)+B(30)+C(30)=80 marking structure the app teaches. No app edit required.

## Bottom line

All five rules **CONFIRMED** against the 2024 HL scheme with the same values as
2025 — the rules are stable across at least two consecutive sittings. **No rule
CHANGED, none NOT PRESENT, no session edit required.**
