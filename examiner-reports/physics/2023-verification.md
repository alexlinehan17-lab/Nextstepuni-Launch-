# Physics HL — 2023 Marking-Scheme Verification (second-year cross-check)

## Purpose
Depth item 5. We already hold the **2025** SEC LC Physics HL marking scheme
(`2025-marking-scheme.pdf` / `.md`). This document cross-checks four cited
marking rules against a **different, earlier** official year — the
**2023 SEC LC Physics Higher Level marking scheme** — to confirm the rules
are stable across years and flag any drift.

## Result: all four rules hold; no drift requiring a session edit

The general marking notes on **p.3–4 of the 2023 scheme are word-for-word identical**
to the 2025 scheme's general notes (points 1–10). The per-question granule marking is
structurally identical. No cited rule changed.

## Notes / caveats on the two soft edges

- **Rule 1 — "each ~3 marks."** The granule *structure* (formula is its own bankable
  granule, independent of substitution and answer) is fully confirmed and stable. The
  "~3 marks" figure is an approximation, not a fixed rule: granule size scales with the
  question. Short Section-B parts (Q6, eight parts) commonly use 2-mark granules
  (e.g. Q6(a) `[2]/[2]/[2]/[1]`, p.11); larger single-value questions bank a bigger
  formula granule (Q6(d) `[4]` formula, Q6(j) `[4]` formula, p.11). So read the cited
  "~3 marks" as a typical case, not a guarantee — this is consistent with the 2025 scheme
  and is not drift.
- **Rule 3 — error carried forward.** The scheme states the "−1 per arithmetical slip"
  half explicitly (note 8), but neither the 2023 nor the 2025 scheme prints an explicit
  "error carried forward / downstream steps still score" sentence in the general notes.
  ECF is *realised structurally*: because each granule (formula, substitution, answer) is
  awarded independently, a slip that costs one granule does not cascade — later granules
  built on the slipped value still score. This is standard SEC application and is
  identical between 2023 and 2025, so there is no drift; but the app should not claim ECF
  is a *written* general rule — it is a consequence of the independent-granule design.

## Anything unverified
- Nothing material unverified. All four rules located verbatim (or structurally, for the
  two soft edges above) with page references. The only wording the schemes do not contain
  is the phrase "error carried forward" itself (see Rule 3 note) — the mechanism is present,
  the label is not.

## Bottom line
No session edit required. The 2023 and 2025 Physics HL schemes carry identical general
marking notes and identical granule logic; all four cited rules are confirmed across both
years. Recommend softening any app copy that presents "~3 marks per granule" as fixed
(it varies 1–6) and any copy that presents "error carried forward" as an explicitly
written SEC rule (it is implied by granule independence, not printed).
