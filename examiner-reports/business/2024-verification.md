# Business 2024 (HL) — Second-Year Verification of App-Cited Marking Rules

## Source

- **Report type:** Marking Scheme (SEC, finalised)
- **Year:** 2024
- **Level:** Higher
- **File:** `2024-marking-scheme.pdf` / `2024-marking-scheme.md`
- **Mirror URL:** https://educateplus.ie/sites/default/files/storage/Business%20HL.pdf (SEC original)
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-07-06
- **Purpose:** Cross-check the five 2025-derived marking rules the app cites against a second recent HL scheme (2024) to confirm they still hold or flag year-to-year change.

**Page refs below are the printed page numbers of the 2024 scheme; these align 1:1 with the PDF sheet index (cover = sheet 1, printed p.4 = sheet 4, etc.). The `.md` uses `<!-- page N -->` markers on the same numbering.**

> **Headline finding:** The underlying *conventions* the app relies on are stable (Name / Explain / Link ABQ template; "no link without theory"; "separate links per section"; List cue rewards naming only; numeric answers marked per-step; chart labels/title carry marks). BUT three of the five rules cite **2025-specific mark counts / question placements that do not recur in 2024**, because the exact grids are set per-question each year. The ABQ "evaluate" component in particular moved (2025: Part B(i), heaviest; 2024: Part C, lightest). None of this makes the 2025 dossier factually wrong for 2025 — but the app should frame these as *conventions illustrated by the 2025 paper*, not as invariant fixed grids. See "Would this require a session edit?" at the end.

## Rule-by-rule verdict

### Rule 1 — ABQ Part (A) grid = "N@5 (2+2+1)" Name+Explain+Link; "No link awarded without relevant theory"; "separate links required per section"

**Verdict: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (conventions confirmed verbatim; the specific `4@5 (2+2+1)` uniform grid is question-specific and differs in 2024).** — p.5

- **Verbatim rules CONFIRMED (p.5):** *"In relation to the links given, they must be a direct relevant quote/phrase/statement from the ABQ indicating that candidates clearly understand the point(s) of theory presented. **Separate links are required in each section. No link awarded without relevant theory.**"* — word-for-word identical to the 2025 scheme (2025 p.6). Stable across both years.
- **Grid differs:** 2024 ABQ Part (A) ("Outline the entrepreneurial skills/characteristics displayed by the owner of New Age Care Ireland Ltd") is marked **`10m(4+3+3) + 5m(2+2+1) + 5m(2+2+1)` = 20**, i.e. a lead 10-mark point plus two 5-mark points — NOT the uniform `4@5 (2+2+1)` seen in 2025. The `(2+2+1)` Name/Explain/Link micro-structure is present on the 5-mark points, so the template is recognisably the same, but "N@5" is not the literal grid this year.

### Rule 2 — The "List" grid pays descending (e.g. 3,3,1,1) and explanation earns nothing on a List cue

**Verdict: PRINCIPLE CONFIRMED; specific descending `3,3,1,1` pattern NOT PRESENT in 2024.** — p.6

- 2024's pure "List" cue is Q1(C)(i) *"List three grounds under which a dismissal can be deemed unfair…"* marked **`3@2m`** (p.6) — a **uniform** 2-marks-per-named-ground grid, not descending. Only naming is paid; there is no explain sub-mark, so **explanation earns nothing on the List cue — principle CONFIRMED.**
- The descending `3,3,1,1` shape in 2025 was a direct consequence of *"List **four** grounds"* for **8** marks; 2024 asks for *three* grounds for 6 marks, hence uniform. The specific numeric pattern is question-dependent, not a fixed rule.
- (For contrast, Q10 Section 1 2024 *"List three PR methods, provide an example for each"* is `4m(2+2)+3m(2+1)+3m(2+1)` (p.21) — descending-ish AND rewards examples, because the cue explicitly asks for an example. Confirms the broader point: the cue drives the grid.)

### Rule 3 — ABQ Part (B)(i) evaluation is the heaviest component (3 of 7 marks)

**Verdict: CHANGED / NOT PRESENT AS STATED in 2024.** — p.5

- In **2025**, Part (B)(i) was *"Evaluate the effectiveness of management control"* marked `4@7 (1+2+1+3)` with the Evaluate component = **3 of 7** (heaviest). — 2025 p.6.
- In **2024** there is **no evaluation question at Part (B)**: Part (B) is a contract question — (i) *"Define the term a contract"* `6m(3+3)` and (ii) *"Discuss the essential elements of a valid contract"* `10m(4+3+3)+2@7m(3+2+2)` = 30 (p.5).
- The 2024 "evaluate" ABQ task is **Part (C)**: *"Evaluate how James's management skills have contributed to the success…"* marked **`3@10m (4+3+2+1)` — Name, contribution explained, link, evaluate** — where **evaluate = 1 of 10 (the LIGHTEST sub-component)**, the inverse of the 2025 weighting (p.5).
- So the claim "Part (B)(i) evaluation is heaviest at 3/7" is a 2025-only fact. Both the *position* (B(i) → C) and the *weight* of evaluation (heaviest → lightest) changed year-to-year. The general point that "an evaluation/judgement sub-mark exists and is separately awarded" holds; the specific weighting does not.

### Rule 4 — Section 1 ratio questions marked as per-step points (e.g. 5 @ 1m); bare answer caps at 1

**Verdict: SPECIFIC FORM NOT PRESENT in 2024 (no Section 1 ratio question); per-step numeric-marking PRINCIPLE CONFIRMED elsewhere.** — p.8

- 2024 **Section 1 contains no ratio/financial-calculation question** — none of the twelve Q grids is a `5@1m` ratio scheme (grids are `3+2+2+2+1`, `4m(2+2)`, `6m(3+3)`, `2@5m(3+2)`, etc., p.4). So the exact "Section 1 ratio, 5@1m, bare answer caps at 1" template does not appear this year.
- The **per-step numeric-marking principle IS present** in 2024, in Section 3 Q2(A)(ii) Balance of Trade / Balance of Payments: *"BOT 6 marks, BOP 8 marks — **formula 1 mark and each correct figure 1 mark, € sign 1 mark, surplus/deficit 1 mark**"* (p.8). Each procedural element is its own mark, exactly the mechanism behind the 2025 `5@1m` note — so a bare answer with no workings forfeits the per-step marks. Principle CONFIRMED; the Section-1 ratio manifestation is 2025-specific.

### Rule 5 — Breakeven chart: calculations-only capped (~12 of 25); labels/title carry marks

**Verdict: NOT PRESENT in 2024 (no breakeven-chart question on the paper).** — p.16

- 2024 has **no breakeven chart-drawing question**. Breakeven appears only as a Section 1 short question, Q4 — a five-part fill-in-the-blank/underline item marked `3+2+2+2+1` = 10 (p.16). There is no `25`-mark chart grid, no "Calculations only" cap, no Title/Axis marks in 2024.
- The 2025 rule (chart 25m; calc-only capped at 12m; Title 2m + Axes 1+1m carry marks) is accurate to the 2025 paper (2025 p.11) but simply did not recur as a question in 2024. The *convention* it illustrates — "a diagram without labels/title loses marks; the drawing is where most marks sit" — is a stable SEC pattern, just not testable against 2024 for this specific chart.

## Would this require a session (app content) edit?

**No factual correction is required — the 2025 dossier is accurate for the 2025 paper.** But the verification surfaces a *framing risk* worth noting for whoever owns the student-facing content:

- Rules 1, 3, 4, 5 as worded read like invariant, every-year marking rules. Against 2024 they are better understood as **conventions instantiated by the specific 2025 questions**. The invariant parts are the *templates and principles* (Name/Explain/Link; "no link without theory"; "separate links per section"; List = name only; numeric answers marked per-step; chart labels/title carry marks). The *exact mark splits and which ABQ part hosts the evaluation* change every year.
- The single item that could actively mislead a student sitting a future paper is **Rule 3** ("evaluation is heaviest, in Part B(i)"): in 2024 the evaluate task was in Part (C) and was the *lightest* sub-mark. If the app tells students "the evaluation is the heaviest component of the ABQ," that is a 2025-only artefact. **Recommended (optional) edit:** reframe Rule 3 to "the ABQ always contains an 'Evaluate' task with its own separately-awarded judgement mark — do not stop at Name/Explain/Link" rather than asserting a fixed 3-of-7 weighting or a fixed Part (B)(i) location.
