# Maths Scale-System Verification — 2024 Higher Level

**Purpose.** Second-year cross-check of the marking scale-system rules the app
teaches for Leaving Certificate Mathematics. We already hold the **2023 OL Paper 2**
scheme and the **2025 Foundation** scheme. This file verifies the same rules against a
*different* year and level: the **SEC Leaving Certificate 2024 Marking Scheme —
Mathematics, Higher Level** (`2024-hl-marking-scheme.pdf`, combined Paper 1 + Paper 2,
56 PDF pages). Page references below use the **printed page label** shown in the PDF
(e.g. `[4]`), with the PDF page index in parentheses.

**Source provenance.** SEC official scheme, mirrored via educateplus.ie. Cover page
(PDF idx 0) reads: "Coimisiún na Scrúduithe Stáit / State Examinations Commission /
Leaving Certificate 2024 / Marking Scheme / Higher Level / Mathematics". Confirmed
genuine SEC (not a mock/Pre-Leaving).

## The "Structure of the marking scheme" page — verbatim quote

Printed page **[4]** (PDF idx 3):

> **Structure of the marking scheme**
> Candidate responses are marked according to different scales, depending on the types
> of response anticipated. Scales labelled A divide candidate responses into two
> categories (correct and incorrect). Scales labelled B divide responses into three
> categories (correct, partially correct, and incorrect), and so on. The scales and the
> marks that they generate are summarised in this table:
>
> | Scale label | A | B | C | D |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | No of categories | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
> | 5 mark scales | 0, 5 | 0, 2, 5 | 0, 2, 3, 5 | 0, 2, 3, 4, 5 |
> | 10 mark scales | | 0, 4, 10 | 0, 4, 6, 10 | 0, 3, 5, 7, 10 |
> | 15 mark scale | | | 0, 6, 8, 15 | 0, 4, 6, 8, 15 |
> | 20 mark scale | | | | 0, 8, 11, 13, 20 |
>
> **Marking scales – level descriptors**
> A-scales (two categories): incorrect response / correct response
> B-scales (three categories): response of no substantial merit / partially correct
> response / correct response
> C-scales (four categories): response of no substantial merit / response with some
> merit / almost correct response / correct response
> D-scales (five categories): response of no substantial merit / response with some
> merit / response about half-right / almost correct response / correct response

**Palette of annotations available to examiners** — printed page **[5]** (PDF idx 4)
gives the credit-descriptor vocabulary the app uses:

> Cross — "merits 0 credit"; **P** — "merits Partial Credit"; **L** — "merits Low
> Partial Credit"; **M** — "merits Mid Partial Credit"; **H** — "merits High Partial
> Credit"; **F star** — "merits Full Credit – 1"; **Star** — "Rounding / Unit /
> Arithmetic error / Misreading"; **Oversimplify** — "The candidate has oversimplified
> the work"; **Work of merit** — "The candidate has produced work of merit (in line
> with that defined in the scheme)".

## Rule-by-rule result

| # | Rule the app teaches | Status | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A/B/C/D scale system (A=2 categories … D=5); credit descriptors "no credit / low partial / mid partial / high partial / full credit" | **CONFIRMED** | Scale table + descriptors on page **[4]** (idx 3); the exact "Low/Mid/High Partial Credit / Full Credit(–1) / 0 credit" vocabulary on page **[5]** (idx 4) palette |
| 2 | "Work of merit" = a correct relevant formula earns the lowest non-zero credit | **CONFIRMED** | Palette defines "Work of merit" on page **[5]** (idx 4); explicit worked example on page **[33]** (idx 32): *"Partial Credit: Work of merit, for example, correct relevant formula, such as P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)"* — i.e. a relevant formula earns the lowest non-zero band. | 3 | Full Credit −1 (denoted *) for rounding / units / misreading / arithmetic slip that does not oversimplify | **CONFIRMED (format changed)** | Conveyed via the **Palette** (page [5], idx 4): "Star — Rounding / Unit / Arithmetic error / Misreading" and "F star — merits Full Credit – 1", with a separate "Oversimplify" annotation carrying the "does-not-oversimplify" nuance. | 4 | An answer without sufficient supporting work is generally awarded the lowest non-zero credit | **NOT PRESENT as a general statement** | No general-guidelines sentence to this effect exists in this scheme (there is no "General Guidelines" page in the 2024 HL combined document). ECF is applied **in practice** per-question, e.g. page [12] (idx 11): "Low Partial Credit • Brings down derivative from (i)" (credit for reusing a prior-part result). The mechanism operates but is not codified as a general rule in this document. |

## Findings / anything needing a session edit

- **Rules 1, 2, 3 hold across a second year AND a second level (2024, Higher).** The
  A/B/C/D scale (2–5 categories), the Low/Mid/High Partial + Full-Credit−1 credit
  vocabulary, the "work of merit = relevant formula → lowest non-zero credit" rule, and
  the `*` = Full Credit−1 rounding/units/misreading rule are all present and unchanged
  in substance. No app content change required for these.

- **Presentation change worth noting (Rule 3):** the 2024 HL scheme moved the `*`/Full
  Credit−1 definition out of the "Structure" prose (where the 2022 HL scheme stated it)
  and into the **Palette of annotations** table. If the app quotes the *paragraph* form
  ("a mark that is one mark below the full-credit mark… denoted with a *"), that phrasing
  is a 2022-era/OL artefact — still accurate, but a reviewer comparing against 2024 HL
  will find it in the palette, not the structure page. No correction needed; flag only.

- **Rules 4 and 5 are NOT stated as general rules in this particular scheme.** They are
  applied per-question rather than declared up front. This does not falsify the app's
  claims (the rules genuinely operate, and other SEC maths schemes with a "General
  Guidelines" page do state them explicitly), but **if the app cites a marking scheme as
  the source for rules 4 and 5, that citation should point to a scheme/year that actually
  states them as general rules — not to this 2024 HL document.** Recommend a
  session-side check that the in-app citation for rules 4/5 resolves to a scheme that
  carries the explicit statement (candidate: the already-filed 2023 OL P2 scheme, or a
  scheme with a numbered "General Guidelines" section).
