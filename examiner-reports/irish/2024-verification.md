# Irish (Gaeilge) HL — 2024 Second-Year Verification

Cross-checks the cited Irish (Gaeilge) Higher Level marking rules — originally
sourced from the **2025** scheme (`2025-insights.md`) — against a **different
year (2024)** to confirm they are stable rules and not one-year artefacts.

## Source

- **Document:** State Examinations Commission (Coimisiún na Scrúduithe Stáit),
  *An Ardteistiméireacht 2024 — Scéim Mharcála, Ardleibhéal, Gaeilge*
  (Leaving Certificate 2024 Marking Scheme, Higher Level, Irish — written papers).
- **Confirmed genuine SEC scheme, not a mock/Pre-Leaving:** cover page (PDF p.1) reads
  *"Coimisiún na Scrúduithe Stáit / An Ardteistiméireacht 2024 / Scéim Mharcála /
  Ardleibhéal / Gaeilge"*; PDF p.2 carries the standard SEC *"Nóta do mhúinteoirí agus
  do scoláirí"* preamble.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-07-06 via educateplus.ie mirror
  (`https://educateplus.ie/sites/default/files/storage/Irish%20HL.pdf`).
- **Filed as:** `2024-marking-scheme.pdf` (56 PDF pages) + `2024-marking-scheme.md`.
- **Page-numbering note:** page refs below are **PDF page numbers**. The internal
  printed page number is 2 lower (PDF p.12 = printed p.10, PDF p.14 = printed p.12,
  PDF p.18 = printed p.16). The 2025 insights cited the *printed* numbers (p.10 / p.12 /
  p.16), so the two documents line up on identical internal pagination.
- **Extraction caveat:** PyPDF2; Irish text and tabular mark panels garble on extraction.
  Quotations below were read against the PDF page images to confirm wording.

## Supporting cross-checks (structure unchanged 2024 → 2025)

- **Paper architecture identical.** Páipéar 1 (160): Cluastuiscint 60 + Ceapadóireacht 100;
  Páipéar 2 (200): Léamhthuiscint 100 + Prós 30 + Filíocht 30 + Litríocht Bhreise 40.
  (PDF p.3 summary banner.)
- **Comprehension Q1–5 = 7 marks each**, split (a) = 3, (b) = 4, with (b)(i) = 2 / (b)(ii) = 2.
  *"Glacfar le freagraí i bhfocail an iarrthóra nó i bhfocail an tsleachta, de réir mar a
  éilíonn an cheist."* Q6(a) grammar = 3 marks (2 for first correct item, 1 for the second).
  All identical to 2025. (PDF p.18 / printed p.16.)
- **Deficient-length caps (S-symbols) identical:** S1 = Stíl 4 / Ábhar 13 / Gaeilge 68;
  S2 = 3 / 11 / 56; S3 = 2 / 9 / 44 (plus an S4 = 5 / 10 / 54 for the A(c)/A(d)/A(e) short
  tasks). Matches 2025. (PDF p.17 / printed p.15.)
- **Aural:** Tuiscint = 60 with *"Bainfear ó 0 go 3 ar chaighdeán na Gaeilge"* — up to 3
  marks stripped for the standard of Irish; 2-mark items with 1-mark partial credit for
  vague retrieval. Matches 2025. (PDF p.3, pp.10–11.)

## Verdict

**No drift.** All three cited rules hold verbatim in the 2024 HL scheme, with identical
mark splits, wording, and internal pagination to 2025. **No session edit required** — the
composition 5/15/80 architecture, the Stíl-0 genre-discipline rule, and the Q6(b) 12-mark
own-words gate are stable, multi-year SEC rules.

**Unverified / out of scope:** the oral (Béaltriail) marking grammar is on a separate SEC
scheme not included in this document, so nothing about the 40% oral component was checked
(same limitation as the 2025 source).
