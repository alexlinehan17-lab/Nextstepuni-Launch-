# SEC Assessment Arrangements 2026 — Leaving Certificate Irish oral (An Bhéaltriail Chomónta)

> Source: *Assessment Arrangements for Junior Cycle and Leaving Certificate Examinations 2026*, 
An Roinn Oideachais agus Óige / Department of Education and Youth, September 2025 (gov.ie). 
Extracted pages: cover, and the Leaving Certificate Irish senior-cycle assessment-structure pages 
(printed pp. 39-40) which set out the official mark allocation for the common oral (An Bhéaltriail Chomónta) 
and the two written papers. This is the SEC's authoritative statement of the oral's 240-mark / 40% 
component allocation; it is the assessment-structure document, NOT the per-question oral marking grid.

The full 100-page all-subject source PDF is staged in the session scratchpad; only the Irish pages are filed here.
