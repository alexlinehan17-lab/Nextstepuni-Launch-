# History 2024 (HL) — Second-Year Verification Cross-Check

**Purpose.** Depth-item-5 second-year verification for Leaving Certificate History,
Higher Level. The 2025 HL marking scheme is already filed
(`2025-marking-scheme.pdf` / `2025-insights.md`). This cross-checks four cited
rules against a **different** year's official SEC scheme to confirm they hold and
to flag any drift.

- Title page carries the *Coimisiún na Scrúduithe Stáit / State Examinations

## Drift assessment

- **Rules 1, 3, 4 — no drift.** Wording and mark values are identical between the
  2025 and 2024 HL schemes (60/40 split; "If only ONE, Max. CM = 50"; "Answer
  referring to one document only = 5M max"). These are stable, verbatim SEC rules
  across both years and can be cited with confidence.
- **Rule 2 — no year-on-year drift, but the citation as phrased is inaccurate to
  the source.** Both the 2024 and 2025 schemes support "OE rewards analysis over
  narrative" **verbatim** via the criterion "(i.e.: more than mere narrative)".
  Neither scheme contains an OE band descriptor reading "relies mostly on
  narrative" — that phrase is a paraphrase of the lower bands, not an SEC quote.
  The two schemes agree with each other, so this is **not drift**; it is a
  paraphrase in the cited rule that should not be presented to students as a
  direct quotation.

## Items needing a session edit

- **Rule 2 wording.** Wherever student-facing content (or a `commonTraps` /
  strategy string) attributes the phrase **"relies mostly on narrative"** to an
  SEC OE band, reframe it. Either (a) quote the actual SEC criterion — *"more
  than mere narrative"* — or (b) keep "relies mostly on narrative" but label it
  clearly as a paraphrase of the lower OE bands (Weak = "fails to answer the
  question as set"; Very weak = "offers only scraps of information"), **not** as a
  verbatim descriptor. This applies equally to the 2025-sourced content, since the
  2025 scheme uses the same wording.
