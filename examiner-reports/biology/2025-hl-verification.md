## Rule-by-rule verification

| # | Cited rule (from 2023 HL scheme) | 2025 HL status | Evidence (page ref) |
|---|----------------------------------|----------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Points-based notation** — `or` separates alternatives when one answer is needed; **solidus `/`** separates each mark-bearing word/term/phrase when multiple are needed; mark in **bold** beside the answer; `5(4)` = five parts × 4 marks each; `2(4) + 4(3)` = first two correct answers 4 marks each, each subsequent 3 marks; word in **brackets `( )`** is not required (contextualises or is an alternative). | **CONFIRMED** — verbatim, all five sub-conventions present and identically worded. | p.3, "How to use the marking scheme" (bullets). Live examples: `6(3)` p.4; `4(3)` p.5; `3(6)+2(1)`-style compound tallies throughout Section A pp.8–14; `Any two of (a),(b),(c),(d) 30,30` Q16/Q17 pp.28–36. |
| 2 | **Context rule** — a key term scores only if presented in a correct statement/context. | **CONFIRMED** — verbatim. "Key words or terms or phrases may be awarded marks, only if presented in the correct context." Worked example unchanged from 2023: "Water is drawn up the xylem by osmosis." → two key terms present (xylem, osmosis) but statement incorrect, so only 3 marks awarded (for the xylem/water-movement reference), not for osmosis. | p.4, example 1. |
| 3 | **Section A surplus penalty** — "a surplus wrong answer cancels the marks awarded for a correct answer" (**Section A only**). | **CONFIRMED** — verbatim, and explicitly flagged "[only in Section A]". Same worked examples: xylem reinforced with "Chitin, lignin" → 4 − 4 = 0; "Carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, calcium" → surplus incorrect cancels one correct → 3(3); cancelled-surplus cases restored to full marks. | p.4 (rule + xylem example) continuing to p.5 (protein-elements examples). |
| 4 | **Sections B/C asterisk nullification** — for single-term answers a wrong alternative nullifies the correct one; each such case marked with `*`. | **CONFIRMED** — verbatim. "In the other sections of the paper (Sections B and C), there may be instances where a correct answer is nullified by the addition of an incorrect answer. This happens when the only acceptable answer is a specific word or term. Each such instance is indicated in the scheme by an asterisk *." Asterisks are used live throughout Sections B and C (e.g. `*Fungi` Q10 p.17; `*Root`, `*Stamen`, `*Carpel`, `*Ovule` Q11 p.18; `*EeNn`, `*Linked` Q12 pp.20–21; `*Deoxyribonucleic acid` Q13 p.21). | p.5 (rule); asterisks in scheme body pp.17–36. A dedicated annotation symbol for "Surplus incorrect answer … has cancelled a correct answer" is listed in the marking-annotations table, p.6. |

**Summary: all four cited rules CONFIRMED with no drift.** Wording, worked examples, section-scoping
(surplus = Section A only; asterisk = Sections B/C) and the `*` mechanism are identical between the
2023 HL (Deferred) scheme and the 2025 HL (main) scheme. The 2023 dossier's rule statements remain
accurate for a second year.

## Section A / Section C question count — correction to 2023 insights

The 2023 insights file (`2023-insights.md`, "best of" table, lines 22 & 24) records:

- Section A = "Best 5 (**of 6**)"
- Section C = "Best 4 (**of 6**)"

**Both counts are WRONG.** The real structure, confirmed in *both* the 2025 HL scheme and (on
re-reading) the 2023 HL scheme itself, is **seven questions in each of Sections A and C**:

| Section | Basis | Questions present | Correct "of N" |
|---------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| A | Best **5** (100 marks) | Q1–Q7 (seven ×20-mark questions) | **best 5 of 7** |
| B | Best **2** (60 marks) | Q8–Q10 (three ×30-mark questions) | best 2 of 3 (already correct) |
| C | Best **4** (`4(60)` = 240 marks) | Q11–Q17 (seven ×60-mark questions) | **best 4 of 7** |

Evidence — 2025 HL: `Section A Best 5 100` p.8 with Question 1…Question 7 pp.8–14;
`Section B Best 2 60` p.15 with Q8–Q10; `Section C Best 4 4(60)` p.18 with Q11–Q17 pp.18–36.
Evidence — 2023 HL (Deferred): identical layout — `Section A Best 5 100` then Q1–Q7;
`Section B Best 2 60` then Q8–Q10; `Section C Best 4 4(60)` then Q11–Q17
(`2023-marking-scheme.md`, section headers at the Q1–Q7 / Q8–Q10 / Q11–Q17 blocks).

Paper total unchanged: 100 + 60 + 240 = **400 marks**. Only the *choice pool* was misreported
(6 → 7 in Sections A and C); the number of answers that count (5 / 2 / 4) is correct.

**Action:** `2023-insights.md` lines 22 and 24 corrected in this same change (6 → 7). Any
student-facing content that repeats "best 5 of 6" / "best 4 of 6" for Biology should be updated
to "best 5 of 7" / "best 4 of 7".
